You're offline

Maranatha is installed on this device and your sign-in is saved. Your lessons and records are never stored in the app download. They stay in the Learning Center's secure account and load as soon as you're back online.

How to use the app

A one-minute walk-through. It plays by itself. Tap pause, back or next any time.

What's new

  • Install it like an app on iPhone, Android or a computer.
  • Stays signed in, even on phones with more than one Google account.
  • Works with no signal: screens you've opened are saved on your device for 14 days.
  • Better emails: grant alerts show each deadline with a days-left count, and one big button opens the app.
  • How to and Help buttons at the top of every screen.
  • Social media setup: tap Help, then Set up social media. Facebook and Instagram, step by step.

Ask the team

Tell us what you were trying to do and what happened. Then choose who to send it to. Your email opens with everything filled in, so you only tap Send.

Set up social media

Step by step for Facebook and Instagram. All the words are written for you. Tap Copy, then paste where the step says. Tap Done after each step. Your place is saved on this device.
